Step 01 — Registration

The athlete becomes a verified identity.

A district-level athlete creates a single profile in the Centralised Sports Repository. ID, certifications, and medical records are uploaded once. The federation verifies. The verified identity is the foundation for every interaction that follows.

Centralised RepositoryModule 01
Verification & NominationModule 02
Step 02 — Accreditation

A digital card that opens the right doors.

The athlete is accredited for the upcoming national meet. A digital accreditation card — encoded with role, access zones and event privileges — appears in their wallet and at every gate. A scan confirms identity and unlocks only the zones they're authorised to enter.

AccreditationModule 04
Attendance & Check-inModule 08
Step 03 — Anti-Doping

Integrity, logged on the spot.

A pre-competition test pulls the athlete in. Sample collection, chain-of-custody and lab results are entered directly into the dashboard — linked to the athlete's accreditation ID. The result is timestamped, cryptographically signed, and visible only to authorised officials.

Step 04 — Competition

The tournament plays itself.

Brackets are generated. Matches are scheduled. Officials record results from courtside tablets. As each result lands, standings update live, certificates are queued for issuance, and the athlete's performance metrics flow back into their permanent record.

Step 05 — Insight

Every data point, a decision.

Coaches open the analytics dashboard the next morning. Performance deltas, attendance, fitness assessments and grant eligibility appear in one view. Selections for the next camp are made from data, not memory. Certificates are auto-issued. The cycle begins again.
