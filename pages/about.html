/ About

Sports needs a system,
not a stack of forms.

GB·GMS is GoBananas' answer to a question federations have been asking for a decade: how do you run a games at national scale without losing the human story underneath?

/ 01
Why we built it

We believe infrastructure
should disappear.

For too long, Indian sports has run on goodwill, paper and improvisation. Talent that should be on a national podium gets lost between district registration and a state federation's inbox. GB·GMS exists to collapse that distance.

We're not building a portal. We're building a piece of public sporting infrastructure — one that's so well-considered, federations stop noticing it. The athlete is what shows up. The story is what stays.

Principles

Five commitments

/ 03 — Architecture

Layered like infrastructure.
Felt like an app.

Layer 01
Experience Surface
Athlete app, coach portal, federation console, venue dashboard, organiser command centre.
Layer 02
Module Engine
18 production modules, each independently versioned and selectively adoptable.
Layer 03
Identity & Trust
Centralised registry, role-based access, signed certificates, cryptographic audit trails.
Layer 04
Data Backbone
Encrypted athlete records, performance lakes, exportable assessment streams.
Layer 05
Platform Integrations
NADA, Khelo India, payment gateways, SMS / WhatsApp, SSO, BI tools.
