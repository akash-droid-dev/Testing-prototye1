  • Search & discovery

  • Nomination workflows
  • Notification system

  • Event calendar

  • FAQ support
  • Feedback collection

  • Access control
  • Status tracking
  • Renewal & revocation
  • Accreditation database

Digital Certificate Issuance

Custom certificate templates, automated issuance, lifecycle tracking and reissuance — integrated with the Athlete Management System for achievement-triggered awards.

  • Template creation
  • Certificate issuance
  • Lifecycle management
  • Notification system
  • Customisation options
  • Athlete system integration

  • Grievance submission
  • Status tracking
  • Resolution audit

Create tournaments, register participants, generate brackets, schedule matches and record results — with digital certificates issued automatically for achievements.

  • Tournament creation
  • Participant registration
  • Bracket generation
  • Match scheduling
  • Results recording
  • Certificate integration

  • Attendance reports

  • Attendance tracking

Booking Management

  • Notification system

Venue & Infrastructure
