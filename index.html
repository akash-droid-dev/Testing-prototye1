A day in the life of GB·GMS

One athlete. One thread.
The whole journey, captured.

Step 01  /  Registration

The athlete becomes a verified identity.

A district-level athlete creates a single profile in the Centralised Sports Repository. ID, certifications and medical records are uploaded once — the system validates, the federation approves, and the verified identity becomes the foundation for everything that follows.

Centralised RepositoryModule 01
Verification & NominationModule 02
Step 02  /  Accreditation

A digital card that opens the right doors.

The athlete is accredited for the upcoming national meet. A digital accreditation card is issued automatically — encoded with their role, access zones and event privileges. At every gate, a QR scan confirms identity and unlocks only the areas they're authorised to enter.

Accreditation ManagementModule 04
Attendance & Check-inModule 08
Step 03  /  Anti-Doping

Integrity, logged on the spot.

A randomised pre-competition test pulls the athlete in. Sample collection, chain-of-custody and lab results are entered directly into the dashboard — linked to the athlete's accreditation ID. The result is timestamped, immutable and visible to authorised officials only.

Step 04  /  Competition

The tournament plays itself.

Brackets are generated. Matches are scheduled. Officials record results from courtside tablets. As each result lands, standings update live, certificates are queued, and the athlete's performance metrics flow back into their permanent record.

Every data point, a decision.

Coaches open the analytics dashboard the next morning. Performance deltas, attendance, fitness assessments and grant eligibility appear in one view. Selections for the next camp are made from data — not memory. Certificates are auto-issued. The cycle begins again.

/ 05 — Architecture

Built like infrastructure.
Used like an app.

GB·GMS is a layered platform — federations get governance at the top, athletes get experience at the bottom, and the data that flows between them is verifiable, exportable and owned by the people it represents.

Layer 01
Experience Surface
Athlete app, coach portal, federation console, venue dashboard, organiser command centre. Every role gets a deliberate interface.
Layer 02
Module Engine
18 production modules: accreditation, tournaments, training, anti-doping, attendance, certificates, grants, assessment and more.
Layer 03
Identity & Trust
Centralised registry, role-based access, digital accreditation, signed certificates and cryptographic audit trails.
Layer 04
Data Backbone
Encrypted athlete records, performance lakes, assessment streams and export-ready reports for federations and analytics partners.
Layer 05
Platform Integrations
NADA, Khelo India guidelines, payment gateways, SMS / WhatsApp, single sign-on, BI tools and federation-specific systems.
